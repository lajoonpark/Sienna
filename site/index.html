Sienna

Your YouTube Music, kept in a hand-painted record shop.

Get Sienna Read the code

Move the cursor through the records. Click to scatter them.

The shop

Down a Tuscan alley

Sienna opens on a narrow street of brick and stucco houses. You pass under an arch, across a little piazza, to the record shop.

Lemon trees by the door

A vinyl sign hangs beside the shop, with crates of records under the windows. This is the same street you walk down in the app.

The doors swing open

They open on their own and you walk in. The sky outside is the sky where you are, and the lamps come on one by one at dusk.

Eight sections, four floors

Here you get a peek into the main hall and lounge. In the app, keep going to the stacks, glasshouse and garden courtyard. Each of your shelves gets its own kind of rack.

Scroll to walk in

The racks

Flip through your shelves

Your Home shelves and your library are racks of records, each record in its own painted sleeve.

Run the cursor along the crate below. The record under it lifts out with a neighbour each side, wearing a paper name tag. Click one to play it.

The suitcase player

Drop the needle

Pick a record and its vinyl slides out of the sleeve and flies onto a little suitcase record player. The tonearm creeps in as the song plays.

Drag the record to scrub, turn the knob, or press its buttons.

What else

Small things, painted by hand

The sky follows your clock

The sky outside the shop is the sky where you are, and the lanterns come on one by one at dusk. This page does the same, the shop above included. Drag the hour to see it.

A mini player at your cursor

Open the suitcase from any app, without opening the shop. It closes when the cursor leaves it.

CtrlAltK

Search without leaving the keys

Songs, albums, playlists and artists. Songs slide out of the suitcase drawer; albums and playlists fan open into records.

CtrlAltJ

Keeps nothing about you

No tracking and no background update checks. Discord Rich Presence is off until you turn it on.

Runs cool

Once the shop is still it stops redrawing the room, and it lowers its sharpness when the computer warms up.
