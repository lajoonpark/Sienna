Sienna

Your YouTube Music, kept in a hand-painted record shop.

Get Sienna Read the code

Move the cursor through the records. Click to scatter them.

The shop

Flip through your shelves

You walk down a Tuscan alley and into the shop. Your Home shelves and your library are racks of records, in eight sections over four floors.

Hover a record and it comes out toward you with a paper name tag. Try it on the rack below.

The suitcase player

Drop the needle

Pick a record and its vinyl flies out of the sleeve and lands on a little suitcase record player. The tonearm creeps in as the song plays.

Drag the record to scrub, or click it to pause.

What else

Small things, painted by hand

The sky follows your clock

The sky outside the shop is the sky where you are, and the lanterns come on one by one at dusk. This page does the same. Drag the hour to see it.

A mini player at your cursor

Open the suitcase from any app, without opening the shop. It closes when the cursor leaves it.

CtrlAltK

Search without leaving the keys

Songs, albums, playlists and artists. Albums and playlists fan open into records.

CtrlAltJ

Keeps nothing about you

No tracking and no background update checks. Discord Rich Presence is off until you turn it on.

Runs cool

Once the shop is still it stops redrawing the room, and it lowers its sharpness when the computer warms up.
